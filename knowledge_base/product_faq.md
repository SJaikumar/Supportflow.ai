# Product support FAQ — Sample company FAQ (fictional)

If a product arrives damaged, attach clear photos, note the order number and describe the issue. Support will review available order details and the relevant refund/replacement policy.

AI-based image analysis is planned for a later stage. It must only describe visible evidence; an image alone cannot establish responsibility or entitlement to a refund.

For missing parts or incorrect products, support may request product-identifying details and offer a human-reviewed resolution.
