# Refund policy — Sample company policy (fictional)

**Demo policy, not a real store commitment.** Returns may be requested within 14 calendar days of delivery for eligible products. Proof of purchase and product condition may be required.

Damaged-on-arrival products should be reported with an order reference and, when possible, photographs. Images support investigation but do not by themselves prove cause or eligibility.

Refunds, payment disputes and policy exceptions require approval from an authorized human. Approval and actual processing are separate steps. The Stage 1 refund simulator does not transfer money.

The support team must not promise that a refund has been issued until the refund record shows completion.
