# Shipping policy — Sample company policy (fictional)

**Demo policy, not a real store commitment.** Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Estimated delivery dates appear in each order record. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

If an order is late, support checks the recorded delivery promise and courier status. Support must not invent tracking scans. An investigation can be opened and a new verified update time communicated.

For lost packages, support staff must verify shipment evidence before authorizing a reshipment or refund.
